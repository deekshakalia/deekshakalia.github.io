## About Me

I am currently pursuing a **Master of Science in Regulatory Affairs** at Northeastern University (September 2024 – July 2026), building on over three years of hands-on experience in pharmacovigilance and regulatory documentation.

My background spans literature monitoring, aggregate safety reporting, and quality review of individual case safety reports (ICSRs). With a clinical foundation in dentistry, I approach drug safety and regulatory compliance from both a scientific and patient-focused perspective.

I am passionate about bridging regulatory frameworks with real-world healthcare needs — supporting innovation through strategic, compliant, and globally informed solutions.
