The **DIA Global Annual Meeting** (June 15-19, 2025) has been nothing short of extraordinary. From hearing insights directly from the FDA Commissioner to attending the Opening Reception and FDA Appreciation Event, every moment reaffirmed my passion for advancing global health through regulatory science.

One of the most inspiring parts of the experience was meeting so many brilliant professionals whose stories truly resonated, it felt like catching glimpses of the future I aspire to build.

## Reconnecting and Representing

A personal highlight was reconnecting with **Dr. Sanjeev Miglani, MD** along with **Mugdha Chopra** and **Arun Ram MD MBA**. Their continued support and encouragement mean a lot. Having been part of an organisation like AWINSA Life Sciences, which has consistently contributed to global platforms like DIA, fills me with pride and purpose.

I also had the pleasure of meeting **Dr. Srividya Narayanan**, who represented Northeastern University at DIA 2025 through both a compelling poster presentation and the Global Student Case Study Competition, where her team was selected as a top 3 finalist and went on to win. Her work, *"Wearables as Digital Endpoints: Real-World Case Studies and Regulatory Challenges in Clinical Research,"* was insightful and truly inspiring to see in action.

At the DIA 2025 "What Connects Us" exhibit

## It All Starts with Volunteering

Over the past few days, I had the privilege of hearing stories from accomplished professionals, many of whom started their own journeys through volunteering. It was a powerful reminder that every big dream has a humble beginning.

Volunteering at DIA not only allowed me to connect with incredible individuals across the pharmaceutical and regulatory world, but also helped me understand diverse career paths and develop meaningful, transferable skills.

As I helped facilitate sessions, I found myself thinking: *"Deeksha, one day, you'll be up there speaking too."* Like Dr. Sanjeev Miglani beautifully said, *"You'll be the inspiration for students and professionals who dream of making it in the U.S."*

A heartfelt thank you to **Jessica Culp** for her support throughout, and to **Julia Mauro**, my amazing room monitor partner. I learned so much from her in just three days. Thank you for being such a great teammate.

Special thanks also to **Alberto Grignolo, Ph.D., FDIA** and **Chris Slawecki**, your encouragement and wisdom made a lasting impact.

---

It's empowering to be surrounded by people shaping the future of life sciences. I'm walking away with renewed energy, deeper knowledge, and meaningful connections and I can't wait to see what's next.

*"It all starts with volunteering."*  ✨
