This week, I had the incredible opportunity to attend the **41st Virtue Insight Pharmacovigilance USA 2026 Conference**, held on May 27-28, 2026 in Boston, MA, and it was nothing short of inspiring.

A heartfelt thank you to Dr. Sanjeev Miglani, MD Mugdha Chopra, and Arun Ram MD MBA for making this possible. Having had the privilege of working with them at AWINSA Life Sciences, they have been more than colleagues, true mentors who continue to open doors and create opportunities even beyond our time working together. I'm incredibly grateful they welcomed me and gave me the opportunity to be part of this experience.

The conference was an outstanding platform for networking and connecting with brilliant minds who bring decades of experience in drug safety and pharmacovigilance.

## Transforming Pharmacovigilance Through Technology

One of the highlights was attending the panel: *"Transforming Pharmacovigilance Through Technology: AI, Automation, and the Future of Drug Safety - Opportunities, Challenges & Lessons Learned."*

A few key takeaways:

**Vikalp Khare** shared the EMEA perspective on AI adoption, emphasising data quality, human-in-the-loop, and guardrails that ensure AI only accesses the data it's supposed to. Patient safety must be engineered into how we deploy AI.

**AI literacy** was a resounding theme. It's not just about automation, it's about understanding what AI can and cannot do, and how it shifts our responsibilities. AI is a catalyst, we still own the judgment.

**Priyanka Chhikara** brought a compelling lens through her take on AI technology and raised the critical issue of *model collapse*, a timely reminder that how a model is trained and deployed is a regulatory and safety question, not just a technical one.

## Signal Management with Veeva

I also had the chance to attend a session on how Veeva is accelerating signal and risk management with their unified safety suite and the demo was truly impressive. Seeing how an integrated platform can streamline end-to-end pharmacovigilance workflows made it very clear where the industry is heading.
